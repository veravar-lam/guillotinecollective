Monetochka (Rue GY 198, Dakar) is a sound-video concert held by the artist duo Monetochka as part of their world tour Moskau–Istanbul–Tripoli–Casablanca–Dakar. Just as their name (Russian diminutive for "coin"), their sound and video installation series accentuate and distort the dual mechanism of perceptual experience.
