Guillotine Collective

Current Return (Rue de Laeken, 2, Brussels) is the long-awaited retrospective of Angela Holwitz on the occasion of her fifty-first birthday. It celebrates her extensive oeuvre — from the early photographic series Objects of Use, to the highly regarded found-image archive You: Myself and I, to the groundbreaking post-digital moving-image experiments entitled #..#. We are deeply honoured to present this rich and daring body of work.
