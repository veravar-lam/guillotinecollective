Bookfair Interrupted (Rue GY 498, Dakar), running under the logo "He's back with a huge cow!", is a yearly international networking event focused on uniting Senegalese and Gambian publishers, alongside diasporic publishers, writers and readers. It is traditionally hosted in our headquarters, Guillotine Collective Gallery, in cooperation with the International Alliance of Independent Publishers and the National Centre for the Book and Reading Senegal.
